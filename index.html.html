Trust & safety

Built so nobody gets scammed by a fake event.

01

Phone OTP on every account

No anonymous sign-ups — every profile starts with a verified number.

02

Aadhaar e-KYC for organisers

Coaches and organisers who charge fees complete e-KYC and carry a verified badge.

03

Payments go to verified accounts

Paid events only route money to organisers who've completed verification.

04

Gated chat, always

Requests must be accepted before a conversation opens — plus report and block.
