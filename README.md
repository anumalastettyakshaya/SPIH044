# KhelConnect
Sports Connect – Smart Sports Networking Platform
📌 About the Project

Sports Connect is a smart sports networking platform designed to connect Players, Coaches, Organizers, and Court Owners in one place.

The platform helps players discover suitable opponents, participate in sports events, showcase their achievements, connect with coaches, and find nearby sports opportunities.

At the same time, coaches can promote their skills and achievements, while organizers and court owners can advertise their events and facilities to reach more players.

🎯 Key Features
🏃 Players
Create a personalized sports profile
Find and connect with players of similar skill levels
Participate in sports events and competitions
AI-powered player compatibility score
Showcase achievements, tournaments, and milestones
Discover coaches, events, and sports facilities
🏋️ Coaches
Create a professional coaching profile
Showcase sports achievements and experience
Display skills and expertise
Promote coaching services
Showcase student achievements
🏟️ Organizers & Court Owners
Advertise sports events and tournaments
Promote available courts and facilities
Reach a larger community of players
Increase event participation and court bookings

The score considers factors such as:
Skill level
Playing experience
Performance
Playing style

This helps players find opponents with a suitable competitive level, by doing AI compatability test.

💡 Innovation
Unlike traditional sports platforms, Sports Connect combines:
Player Networking + AI Matching + Coach Promotion + Event Discovery + Court Promotion
into a single ecosystem.
